This part was really interesting to us: Remember when cloud computing was supposed to save everyone money? (5:17) Over time, the bills have added up…and many of us are locked in (what some call “cloud rents”, equating it to a technofeudal system where the Mag 7 are our digital Lords of London).

Well, AI pricing is following the same economic pattern—and Andreas explains what to do about it. Key insight (9:41): Even if vendors raise prices, inflation raises labor costs, too. As long as you're getting positive ROI, it's still worthwhile.

His advice: Focus on business value and ROI, not just cost. Don't just look at total spend: look at value per line item and measure tangible outcomes (like successful resolutions, revenue impact)
