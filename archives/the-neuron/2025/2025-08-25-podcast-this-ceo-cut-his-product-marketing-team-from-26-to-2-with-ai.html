Welcome, humans.

A CEO just told his entire 3,500-person company something that made his CMO have nightmares about turning her kids into AI algorithms.

No, seriously—she texted him about it.

Henry Schuck, founder and CEO of ZoomInfo, didn't mince words when he decided to transform his company into an AI-first powerhouse. His message was simple: lean into AI or watch yourself slide down the KPIs bar while others pass you with less effort.

In our latest podcast episode, Henry reveals exactly how ZoomInfo went from a traditional data company to an AI-driven GTM intelligence machine—including the uncomfortable truths most leaders won't admit.

The spiciest moments (with timestamps):

  • (16:43) The French fries analogy: "If there's a job that takes French fries from the fryer and sprinkles salt on them, someone is trying to build a robotic arm to do that."

  • (13:34) How they automated product marketing: From 26 people translating product info into content, down to 2 people managing AI agents.

  • (19:52) The phrase Henry says most on Slack: "I don't ever want to ask you this question again. Put it in an agent."

  • (27:32) When you know the AI pressure is working: His CMO literally dreamed she disappointed him because her kids weren't AI algorithms yet.

  • (35:28) The ticking time clock warning: "You could decide you're not going to do it, but if you don't, you have a ticking time clock on your ability to be a valuable employee."

The actionable insights:

  • (21:04) How to hire for AI talent: Look for systems thinkers—people who can trace a broken home entertainment system back through every connection.

  • (26:48) The moment Henry went all-in: At 40, worried about losing his edge, a friend told him "It's AI. It's 100% AI." They abandoned their entire platform integration project.

  • (33:28) The Cursor moment: "I'm not a coder but a month ago I got on Cursor for the first time... my brain looks at things differently."

  • (38:32) The unglamorous secret to AI success: "It's not the most glamorous work, but the minute you get that [data] infrastructure there, creativity abounds."

The hard numbers:

  • 2/3 of ZoomInfo employees use their AI chatbot daily.

  • 1,000+ internal AI agents created by employees.

  • 20%+ YoY growth in their fastest-growing business unit.

  • 80% of engineering teams pivoted to AI-first products.

Bottom line: Henry isn't threatening people to use AI—he's just telling them the truth. And the truth is uncomfortable.
