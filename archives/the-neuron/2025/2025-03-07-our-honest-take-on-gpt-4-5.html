Our HONEST take on GPT-4.5…

ChatGPT’s newest AI model, GPT-4.5 came out about a week ago as a research preview, and the initial reviews were mixed. We decided not to cover it in depth because we thought it wouldn't be worth it if so few people could actually use it.

That’s changed. OpenAI just rolled out GPT-4.5 so anyone with a Plus subscription can use it up to ~50 times per week.

Here’s how we tried it out: First, we asked GPT-4.5 to write our entire newsletter for us, with no context, using only our style guide and GPT-Search.

To be fair, this is NOT how we write the newsletter; we write section by section, outlining and writing draft v1, then use AI to improve and edit our work as necessary.

Should you use it?

If your work involves persuasion, relationship-building, or emotional finesse, GPT-4.5 appears to be worth trying. For purely analytical tasks, existing GPT-4o or reasoning-focused models like Claude 3.7 might still be preferable depending on your specific needs.

But it is interesting to note that when humans directly compare outputs in blind tests, GPT-4.5 consistently ranks #1 in the LM Arena (or tied with with Grok-3) across multiple categories. That includes coding, math, and hard problems—things it wasn’t supposed to be good at.

Here's our HONEST TAKE: GPT-4.5 might not be a better model, but it’s certainly more fun to talk to. From that standpoint, this model could actually have a lot of staying power for die-hard GPT lovers. Put another way—the “vibes” are real.
