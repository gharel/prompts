Sam just released the OpenAI roadmap for the next few months… here’s what it means for YOU.

You might not remember this, but there used to be a time when ChatGPT was just...ChatGPT. No model picker, no settings to fiddle with, just pure AI magic.

Well, OpenAI is FINALLY fixing the confusing mess of GPT models—and making GPT-5 free(ish).

In a surprise announcement, Sam revealed OpenAI's detailed roadmap for GPT-4.5 (Orion) and GPT-5:

  • GPT-4.5 (Orion) will arrive in a few “weeks” as the last “traditional” model.

  • GPT-5 will follow in a few “months”, and will include o3 on top of it.

  • Separately, OpenAI announced o1 and o3 mini now support files + images.

The interesting thing here is how OpenAI is simplifying by unifying everything—GPT-5 will come fully equipped with voice chat, image creation, search, and Deep Research in one seamless interface.

Here's what this means for you: No more juggling between different models or tools—just tell GPT-5 what you want to do, and it'll handle the rest.

  • Want a weather update? Instant answer.

  • Building a website? It'll methodically write and test the code.

  • Need research help? It'll search the web and cite sources.

GPT will technically do all of this now…but it takes some *~finesse~* to pull off.

Now, the pricing shakeup is even more interesting:

  • Free: Unlimited use of GPT-5 at “standard intelligence.”

  • Plus users: “higher intelligence” version.

  • Pro users: “even higher” intelligence—we talking AGI here, Sam?

But wait, there's more. Sam also had good news for Deep Research—plus and free users will get access to it:

  • Plus: 10 searches/month (scaling up over time).

  • Free: 2 searches/month.

  • Pay-as-you-go option planned, too.

Since Deep Research was reserved for the Pro level of ChatGPT, many of you probably haven’t used it yet—but now that it’s opening up, we’ll share some advice on how to use it in our “Prompt Tip of the Day” below.

Our take: OpenAI is finally admitting what we've all been thinking—their product lineup got waaaay too complicated.

Using ChatGPT lately feels like playing a slot machine—you keep pulling different levers (GPT-4o, GPT-4o with scheduled tasks, o1, o3-mini, o3-mini-high), hoping you'll hit the jackpot with the right combination.

Just look at this mess.

Fun fact: Three Mile Island’s near-miss disaster happened because of confusing controls—user friendly tech saves lives!

After years of increasing complexity, making GPT-5 both free and simpler is an OBVIOUS play to make AI more mainstream. We’ll see if it works!
