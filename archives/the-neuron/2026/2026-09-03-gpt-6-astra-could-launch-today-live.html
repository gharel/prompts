Why Astra is a big deal

Astra is not a random model rumor. OpenAI calls it its next major model, and says it is the first OpenAI system to reach the Critical cybersecurity capability threshold under its Preparedness Framework.

  • OpenAI says Astra represents a significant jump in cybersecurity capability.

  • The company says it has built stronger safeguards specifically because of what the model can do.

  • OpenAI has already started publicly previewing how it evaluated Astra ahead of release.

And then there’s the naming mystery: people are watching for whether Astra becomes GPT-6, stays Astra, or ships under some other name entirely.
