Apple Intelligence is out…for developers, anyway.

Remember the excitement of snooping for presents as a kid, only to have to wait 24 hours before opening them?

That's what Apple just did with its “preview” release of Apple Intelligence on iOS 18.1. The AI tools are here, but only for devs, and only partially.

Oh yeah, and if you’re a regular user, you have to wait until October to get them. 

If you’re a dev, here’s what’s available today: 

  • Writing suggestions, like proofread, rewrite, and tone adjustments (e.g.,”friendly”, “professional”, or “casual”). 

  • AI summaries for email, texts, and voicemails.

  • Enhanced photo search and movie creation.

  • Improved, glowy Siri with troubleshooting advice and better listening. 

Here’s what this actually looks like in practice:

Devs can sign up for the waitlist here, but there's a second waitlist for the AI tools. Fortunately, the onboarding process is reportedly pretty fast (unlike OpenAI’s SearchGPT waitlist).

What's not ready yet:

  • Image and emoji generation (“genmojis” as Apple calls them). 

  • Object removal from photos.

  • ChatGPT integration.

  • Siri's ability to use personal info and act within apps.

Those last two features are crucial—Allowing an AI to take actions inside iPhone apps is the unlock that could completely change the way we interact with our devices—imagine telling Siri to order you an Uber home and it does it without you having to click on the app.

Hopefully, these features will roll out soon, even if the full update isn't ready by October. In the meantime, we’ll be compulsively clicking refresh on YouTube for when MKBHD’s review of Apple Intelligence drops…

P.S. Interestingly, Apple trained these new AI models on Google's TPU chips—not Nvidia’s.
