Elon Musk’s team at xAI just powered on the “World's Most Powerful AI Training Cluster.”

And of course it happened at ~4:20am. It’s Elon. 

Elon’s AI company, xAI, began training on its massive new Memphis Supercluster Monday morning. The goal? Create the “world's most powerful AI” by December 2024. 

If you don’t know what a supercluster is, it’s basically a massive network of Nvidia GPUs (computer chips) working together as a single unit to solve “super” complex calculations at unprecedented speeds. 

And this Memphis Supercluster is the most “super” supercluster we’ve ever seen. The new facility, dubbed the “Gigafactory of Compute”, is a beast:

  • 100,000 liquid-cooled Nvidia H100 GPUs on a single RDMA fabric (for context, Google snagged only 50,000 H100 GPUs last year).

  • Up to 150 megawatts of electricity usage per hour—enough for 100K homes.

  • At least one million gallons of water per day to keep cool! 

As some shared on X, this likely exceeds the capabilities of current top supercomputers like Frontier and Aurora by more than two times. The news also inspired some debates over who in tech had the higher GPU counts. What is this, grade school?  

So is bigger always better? Big tech thinks so. Their logic is: better AI models = more compute = bigger supercomputers = bigger superclusters. 

And with bigger superclusters, you also create better models faster. Case in point: a system like xAI’s could theoretically train GPT-4 in a week, while some estimate the real model took three months to train

That’s why, with the Memphis Supercluster now online, Elon Musk thinks Grok 3 will become “the most powerful AI by every metric” by EOY, despite the fact that Grok 2 hasn’t even been released yet (set to launch next month). 

What to expect: Better models, more frequently. That’s been the trend, at least—look at how the last few model releases have become more squished together.
