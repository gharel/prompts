GPT-5, or Orion, or something else is coming out in December.

That’s right folks, the successor to GPT-4, whether it will actually be called GPT-5 or not, is rumored to be released in early December.

Let’s review the facts on the ground:

  1. OpenAI’s next frontier model is codenamed Orion.

  2. Orion is supposedly 100x more powerful than GPT-4, and is being trained on synthetic data from o1.

  3. Orion will first be released to partner companies; for example, rumors are that Microsoft will get access to Orion as soon as November.

Here’s the thing though—OpenAI says it’s NOT “releasing a model code-named Orion this year.” Sam called the report “fake news.” But if this news is so fake, why did Sam post this cryptic tweet a little over a month ago?

Now here’s the REAL question: with the success of o1, do we really need GPT-5 right now? Don’t get us wrong, GPT-5 would be amazing to have.

I guess the real test will be, what can GPT-5 / Orion accomplish that current AI models can’t? By that we mean, can it:

  • Reduce hallucinations to >0.01%?

  • Maintain its memory for longer, and solve the lost in the middle problem?

  • ACTUALLY reason?

If so, then it’ll definitely be worth it.
