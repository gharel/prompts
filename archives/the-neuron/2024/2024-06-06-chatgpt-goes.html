What ChatGPT’s power outage really means for AI.

On Tuesday, Gen Z and Millennials experienced the most traumatizing event since the great Snapchat redesign of 2018: ChatGPT stopped working for several hours.

And so did Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini. No one knows exactly how it happened, but all four services resumed later that day.

The term traumatizing is, of course, an exaggeration, but ChatGPT’s first outage in over six months really showcased how deeply millions depend on AI chatbots to help them do their jobs these days.

People humorously (& fictitiously) rushed to X to lament that they couldn’t get any work done without ChatGPT. It was if a real power failure had occurred.

That’s just on X, though.

Back in the real world, most didn’t notice that ChatGPT went down cause, TBH, most people don’t use ChatGPT. And that’s sometimes easy to forget in our little AI bubble, where everyone uses and talks about AI 24/7.

Check out these stats:

  • According to a Common Sense Media survey, only 4% of Americans aged 14-22 use generative AI “almost daily or every day.”

  • And Pew reported back in March that just 20% of US adults say “they have ever used ChatGPT for tasks at work.”
