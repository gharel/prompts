Chatbots rule, & ChatGPT is king.

Now, ladies and gentlemen, the moment you've eagerly anticipated... the most celebrated AI tools of 2024:

ChatGPT is by far the most used AI tool, commanding more traffic than the next 10 biggest tools combined, according to SimilarWeb.

People love using *chatbots*, with Bard, Bing, Character AI, and Perplexity rounding out the top 5 (find the top 150 here).

FlexOS

Two shockers: Grammarly at #6 and the amount of widely popular education tools previously unknown to us, such as Brainly, CourseHero, and ELSA.

Not all innovations hyped up by media/tech circles have made a significant impact (yet):

  1. AI for data analysis is limited, and in our experience, ChatGPT hallucinates a lot with numbers (for best results, use the excel or Data Analyst GPT).

  2. Text-to-video: Innovations like Runway and Pika are impressively cool, yet they haven't reached the quality of professionally edited videos.

  3. AI agents: The vision of AI systems capable of performing multiple tasks autonomously remains unfulfilled. Most AI tools excel in a single function.
