OpenAI is prepping the next version of ChatGPT.

A year ago, amid the buzz of ChatGPT-4's release, we began to worry that AI might be advancing too fast for the rest of the world to keep up.

The thought was: if ChatGPT-4 launched shortly after ChatGPT-3.5, then we could see a version far superior to GPT-4 emerge just as quickly.

So we waited. And waited. Yet no ChatGPT-5.

Instead, a year later, we got ChatGPT-4o, a significant upgrade over GPT-4, though not as drastic as the leap from GPT-3.5 to GPT-4.

created by ChatGPT-4o

Yesterday, OpenAI confirmed that it’s actively training the next version of ChatGPT. 

The model’s name is TBD—OpenAI hinted that it might not be GPT-5—but it’s teased by Sam Altman and others to blow all other models out of the water in multiple dimensions.

We also know that it’s being trained on Microsoft hardware orders of magnitude more powerful than previous hardware:

Why it matters: No one knows the exact arrival of this model, but we're eyeing early 2025, considering:

  1. OpenAI likes to take its sweet time between model releases.

  2. Training and fine-tuning new models typically take at least several months each.

  3. GPT-4o launched about a year after GPT-4, following a similar gap after GPT-3.5.

This suggests that ChatGPT-4o, with its full voice and vision capabilities, will likely be the best AI available through the end of 2024.

Also coming with GPT-5: New safety efforts, according to OpenAI.

It’s forming a new Safety and Security Committee featuring Board members Bret Taylor, Adam D’Angelo, Nicole Seligman, and Sam Altman (shocker) to ensure that the AI it’s building doesn’t kill us all.

Of course, this all comes less than two weeks after OpenAI dissolved its long-term AI risks team after both of the team’s leaders resigned. Jan Leike, one of the leaders who resigned and has since criticized OpenAI’s commitment to AI safety, just joined Anthropic.
