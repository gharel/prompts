Moderna sees results with ChatGPT Enterprise.

Remember Moderna, the biotech trailblazer behind the second-best Covid-19 vaccine? We're partial to Pfizer, sorry... But hey, at least Moderna's not J&J!

Well, the now $42B company is partnering with OpenAI to accelerate the development of other life-saving mRNA medicines and, of course, to increase the productivity of its staff.

Two highlights from the partnership reveal:

  1. 100% of Moderna’s legal team is using ChatGPT Enterprise (this plan doesn’t use your data to train its models).

  2. Moderna is seeing huge adoption for GPTs, aka specialized versions of ChatGPT.

In fact, Moderna and its employees (with OpenAI’s assistance) have built over 750 GPTs!

  • One GPT called “Dose ID” reviews clinical data and visualizes large datasets.

  • Another, “Policy Bot GPT”, helps employees get quick answers about internal policies without needing to search through endless documents.

  • A corporate GPT translates biotech jargon into layman's terms for investor updates.

  • Our fave, Contract Companion GPT, can “read” any contract:

GPTs can help automate any repetitive processes or tasks typically reserved for interns. A smart move by Moderna was to encourage leaders AND staff to craft specialized GPTs for their unique needs and share them with colleagues.

To get started using GPTs, start with our guide, OpenAI’s guide, and then experiment with the GPT Builder in ChatGPT+.

1. Firefly 3. This is their latest AI image creation tool, which you can try for free here or in Photoshop.

The quality is…fine (it wouldn't stump you at Thursday Trivia). It's noticeably inferior to Midjourney v6, as evident here:

Also, why did Adobe’s marketing team show a Firefly 3 image that looks more AI-generated than the Firefly 2 image?!

The good thing about Firefly, though, is its integration into nearly all of Adobe's design products, like Photoshop, which is now used by over 90% (!) of the world’s creative professionals.

Firefly also gets paired with handy design features like Generative Fill, which allows you to expand images, alter backgrounds, or even insert objects into an image with a brief description.
