Sam Altman on how to stay relevant in the AI revolution.

OpenAI CEO Sam Altman shared important advice for startups and enterprises: assume ChatGPT will get much better from here on out.

For startups, this translates to building something that won’t be made obsolete by a better ChatGPT. Otherwise, ChatGPT-5 will “steamroll” you (in Uncle Sam’s own words).

For enterprises, it's about crafting your AI strategy with GPT-5/6/7 in mind, and continuously exploring the latest models and tools.

Sam shared another tip on his 20VC podcast appearance: start deploying AI across your company NOW, even if its immediate application seems unclear.

Think of it as mining for gold: the initial ROI may be murky, but you need to excavate with the tools available to discover what AI can do (trust us, it’s a lot).

Then, assess its impact on speed, quality, creativity, and employee productivity—not just on cost savings.
