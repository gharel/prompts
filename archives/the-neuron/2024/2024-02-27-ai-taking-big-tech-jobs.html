Big Tech shows AI is just as good.

Source

Big Tech is proving an uncomfortable new reality: automating customer-facing teams using AI is totally going to happen.

Meta and Google, tired of seeing their employees' "day in the life" TikToks featuring poolside lounging and green smoothies, are trimming the fat.

And it's working.

Digiday reported that Meta's big automation push reduced the # of employees managing a customer’s ad campaigns from 10 down to just 2-3, with big strategy questions going to those 2-3 humans and the remaining nitty-gritty handled by AI chatbots.

You might be thinking, "Surely this must mean worse service?" And yeah, initially the customers were a little peeved.

However, it turns out that, much like those seldom-used personal concierges from high-end credit cards, companies mostly have account teams to say them have them, not cause they’re actually that useful.

Even worse, one Meta advertiser said they didn't actually know what their account reps did!

They were making TikToks, duhh...

Here’s what this means for businesses:

  • Incorporating AI chatbots for customer support is not only feasible but becoming more accepted by consumers.

  • Do a deep audit of how much work your customer teams actually do and how much can be transitioned to self-service platforms.

  • Don’t aim for *full* automation; just start with the basics for now.
