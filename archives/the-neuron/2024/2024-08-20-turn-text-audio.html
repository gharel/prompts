ElevenLabs' new app is trying to “podcastify”everything.

Imagine a world where any text, in any language, can be instantly transformed into natural-sounding speech. That’s basically what ElevenLabs is trying to do with its new Reader app.

ElevenLabs’ wants to expand your audio library beyond podcasts and audiobooks to include any article, PDF, or ebook on your phone—and yes, that includes newsletters like us!

BTW, this isn’t sponsored content, it’s just friggin’ cool—try it on iOS here, and Android here.

The app has been around since June, but just expanded to support an impressive 32 languages with a vast library of voices—even those of iconic celebs.

Some cool use cases: 

  • Judy Garland can teach you biology while walking to class. 

  • James Dean can narrate your steamy romance novel.

  • Sir Laurence Olivier can read you today’s newsletter—just paste the web link and enjoy! 

This new app is powered by ElevenLabs' advanced Turbo v2.5 AI voice model. 

This same tool is available via API (an easy way to connect to it in your own application), which is already being used to power voice interactions for various platforms like the Rabbit r1 device, Perplexity, and more. 

Plus, the company announced the API is 50% cheaper… making it more affordable to roll out high quality text-to-speech for all kinds of products.

Why it's important: ElevenLabs shared how major Youtubers are using its dubbing services to expand their content into new regions with voices that actually sound like them (thanks to ElevenLabs’ ability to clone voices). 

Oh, and BTW, it’s estimated that up to 20% of the population may have dyslexia. So providing people an option to listen to (instead of read) content, in their own language, wherever they go online can only help increase engagement and communication.

Win-win if you ask us!
