No One Got Hurt, Microsoft & OpenAI Say 🤷

Microsoft’s reputation for being ‘anti-soft’ isn’t without reason:

The company, along with Github and OpenAI, is fighting back against a proposed class action accusing Github’s Copilot of illegally scraping licensed code.

  • Copilot is like Grammarly for coders: it uses AI to auto-fill and suggest lines of code for programmers.

  • The legal battle originated in November when programmer and lawyer Matthew Butterick accused Copilot of spitting out lines of code without providing credit.

But now, Microsoft and OpenAI are striking back, saying the complaint “fails on two intrinsic defects: lack of injury and lack of an otherwise viable claim.”

pshhh, classic lawyer jargon.

Basically, the well-paid lawyers at Microsoft claim that there’s no evidence that the coders they took code from were personally harmed.

In other words, they may have been roughed up, but no harm was done.
