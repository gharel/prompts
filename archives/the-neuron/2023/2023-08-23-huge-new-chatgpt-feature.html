Businesses Can Now Train ChatGPT On Their Data.

OpenAI is letting users upload their data to ChatGPT-3.5 so it can execute use cases specifically tailored to your business.

In other words, fine-tuning.

Fine-tuning is when you customize an AI with instructions specific to you, such as marketing material, writing samples, or code formatting. Think of it as setting your preferences on a dating app…

Fine-tuning ChatGPT-3.5 trains it to perform a task in a very particular way, like writing or coding in your brand's distinct style. (Heads up: Fine-tuning ChatGPT-4 is arriving this fall).

Here’s our take: Great prompt engineering is still good enough for handling most tasks.

You’ll want to fine-tune ChatGPT when you require an output that's so specific, it can't be simply described in a prompt.

For example, in customer support, you'll need to fine-tune ChatGPT with:

  • Hundreds of real support examples.

  • Product documentation.

  • Thousands of FAQs.

If this sounds like you, here’s OpenAI’s full fine-tuning guide. But two things to keep in mind:

  1. It’s expensive — 8x more expensive than just using ChatGPT-3.5 (the base model).

  2. Data privacy — Although OpenAI says it won’t misuse your business’ data, some users are dubious.
