Many Companies’ Data Is Too Disorganized To Train AI.

Yeah, it’s like that.

AI is like a car, and data is the gasoline.

Just as a car needs a lot of gas AND a nearby gas pump, an AI model requires not just tons data to train on, but also data that's readily accessible.

This is why, according to S&P’s survey of 1,500 AI decision-makers, data management is the #1 challenge for companies that wanting to implement AI at work. It’s even bigger than security, which is saying something.

The main issue is their data is scattered all-over-the-place, in various formats, and without labels (think: library without a catalog). Getting this data in order can take years, say IT leaders.

So here are a few immediate steps companies should consider (ASAP):

  • Hire experts in data governance (or enlist consultants).

  • Consolidate all your data on the public cloud (the most common starting place for AI pioneers).

  • Clean and label all your data (here’s a guide).

  • Start training models on your data (e.g., with Amazon SageMaker).

Time to clean your data like you'd scrub a public toilet at Exxon Mobil!

You Can Now Modify Parts Of Images On Midjourney.

Midjourney, the top AI art platform, now offers inpainting, which lets users modify a portion of an image they wish to change.

The feature is similar to Adobe’s Generative Fill, except you still have to work with images generated by Midjourney, which is just fine because they're spectacular.

Here’s how the feature works:

select the region you want to change → describe what you want to add.
