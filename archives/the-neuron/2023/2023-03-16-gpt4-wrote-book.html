Mark Zuckerberg Is Going All In On AI 

Everyone, and we mean everyone, is pivoting to AI.

Zuck is no different. Despite pouring billions into metaverse investments over the past few years, the Meta CEO announced in its earnings call that AI is now the company's #1 priority

"Our single largest investment is in advancing AI and building it into every one of our products."

Mark Zuckerberg

While Meta isn't becoming an AI company per se, it is hinting at future AI integrations to improve all its products, including the metaverse.

Why it matters: It's the same as companies being forced to shift to the internet over the past decade to stay relevant and survive. AI will be the next wave.
