One App To Rule All Chatbots? 💬

Most of you know Quora for its Q&A services. The site was a big hit years ago but hit a wall when people ran out of questions to ask (kidding, kinda).

Since ChatGPT's arrival, Quora has quickly pivoted into AI by placing bets behind its new app called Poe.

Poe lets you try models like ChatGPT, GPT-4 and Claude (the latter two cost $20/mth) from under one roof.

You can also create and share your own chatbots by building prompts - like this Japanese tutor chatbot.

The mission stems from Quora's CEO Adam D'Angelo's belief that there will be millions of chatbots for different purposes but that creating those chatbots will be hard. Too hard.

The vibes? It's early. We're excited to see what type of chatbots people create.

D'Angelo also sits on OpenAI's board, so we're curious to see how he develops this given his insider knowledge. For now, it's a good way to experiment with Claude before it's publicly available.
