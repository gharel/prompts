Our honest review of Elon Musk’s “Grok”.

Elon Musk’s ChatGPT competitor “Grok” is finally live for all X Premium+ subscribers in the US and we took it for a test drive.

The result: Grok is just mid.

(mid like a cold ham and cheese sandwich—no crust.)

Its performance is roughly the same as ChatGPT-3.5—the free version—with a few notable exceptions. It’s:

  • noticeably slower.

  • more personable.

  • less likely to say, “I can’t answer that.”

  • capable of tapping into live info from X (but often pulls outdated tweets in our tests).

“Grok is just chatGPT after one too many drinks.”

Joshua Franson

It’s actually so similar to ChatGPT-3.5 that some speculated Grok was trained on ChatGPT’s responses, though this was debunked by an xAI engineer (plus OpenAI bans such practices).

Why it matters: Chatbots at ChatGPT-3.5’s level are becoming commodities, much like toilet paper, and thankfully, not the sandpaper kind…

The big question is whether ChatGPT-4 will follow this trend.

So far, no company has matched GPT-4, except maybe Gemini in theory. Grok doesn’t even come close, which is why we suggest avoiding the $16/month subscription for now.

But don't write off Grok just yet. They’ve got a treasure chest of proprietary data from X and Tesla that could help distinguish itself from its rivals.

5 custom GPTs to put on your radar.

when you get booted from your own company lol

We initially hyped up GPTs, custom versions of ChatGPT, as the “next big thing”.

Fast forward, and not that many people are using GPTs—they’re a bit slow and hard to create, plus OpenAI delayed its App Store for GPTs. 

We’re still bullish.

We created custom GPTs for a major real estate firm that employees enjoy, plus many Reddit users have reported creating GPTs more helpful than GPT-4.

The key is feeding GPTs specialized knowledge that ChatGPT doesn’t have: think APIs, legal contracts, company manuals, GitHub repositories, customer support queries, and the like.

It's about pinpointing repetitive tasks, figuring out what data could help ChatGPT, and adding feedback loops to help the GPT improve.

In the meantime, here are some popular GPTs (full rankings here):

  • Grimoire: a powerful coding assistant.

  • ResearchGPT: research assistant with access to 200M papers.

  • DesignerGPT: creates and hosts websites.

  • Data Analysis: analyze and visualize data.

  • ChatGPT Classic: the OG version of ChatGPT with no updates.
