For Enterprises, Security Is Top Of Mind For AI 🔓

Most business executives believe that AI will impact their orgs, but they’re chilling in the ‘wait-and-watch’ lounge for now.

What’s keeping them on the fence? Two words: Privacy and security. ChatGPT, as enticing as it may be, just doesn’t cut it for the needs of most large organizations.

Enterprise SaaS providers know this and are working hard to develop AI solutions that ensure, “what happens in your business, stays in your business.”

Yesterday, Salesforce unveiled AI Cloud, bringing AI features across all Salesforce applications: Einstein, Data Cloud, Tableau, Flow, & MuseSoft. The drill?

fuse enterprise data from all sources → discover new insights → automate workflows.

Kinda like Microsoft Copilot for your CRM. Cool, but nothing they haven’t teased before.

The real crowd-pleaser is Salesforce’s new Einstein Trust Layer, which promises to keep all sensitive data hush-hush.

Meanwhile, Salesforce’s rival, Oracle, announced its own AI cloud solution with Cohere that offers custom language models while respecting data privacy.

Why it matters: Enterprises might be a tad slow to the AI party compared to consumers (looking at you, AWS Bedrock), but when they do hit the floor, we expect to see a big boost in productivity.

Tip: Using ChatGPT in Teams 🧑‍🤝‍🧑

The secret to writing good prompts is context, and lots of it.

  • Can’t get ChatGPT to come up with content that actually fits your brand? Give it more brand guidelines.

  • Want it to help you brainstorm more and better ideas? Give it more detail about your situation.

But to get similar-quality results across team members, you’ll need some way to share all that context you put in.

Luckily, ChatGPT’s new share links feature lets other people kick off a new chat using yours as a starting point.

Our tip for today: use share links to pre-load context for your teammates. For example, in marketing:

  • Create a new chat and dump in a lot of info about your company/brand, products, tone/voice guidelines and key personas you target.

  • Then, create a share link, bookmark it and send it to your teammates.

  • Now, every time you want to write marketing content, the share link has all the context you need, ready to go.

It’s a “set up once, use it forever” approach!
