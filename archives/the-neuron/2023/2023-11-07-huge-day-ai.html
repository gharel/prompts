GPTs—Create A Chatbot For Anything Imagineable.

IMHO, the standout announcement from OpenAI’s DevDay yesterday was GPTs—build a custom ChatGPT for any use case with zero code.

Until yesterday, ChatGPT was one-size-fits-all.

The problem here is that you had to start from scratch for every new task—plus figure out prompt engineering, which is annoying AF.

Enter GPTs. Now, all you have to do is describe the type of chatbot you want, upload any relevant files, and presto – you can concoct:

  • A mentor for creative writing.

  • A consultant for tech support.

  • A negotiation strategist.

Plus & Enterprise users get access to all these new chatbots for free via the [Explore] feature.

This includes a Canva GPT that designs anything from prompts + a Zapier-powered GPTs that do all y’all business automations within ChatGPT.

The cool part is that soon anyone can design and launch their own GPT, and get paid when people use it. We recently chatted about Poe doing a version of this…RIP!

GPTs are about to be everything everywhere all at once.

This means businesses will build private/public GPTs for very specific functions, from drafting and scrutinizing legal compliance documents to creating tailored sales agreements for clientele.

OpenAI Made It Easier And More Powerful To Build With Its APIs:

While most of us use OpenAI for ChatGPT, >2M developers build new products on top of OpenAI’s APIs.

Yesterday = Christmas for these people.

The main present is a new, smarter model called GPT-4 Turbo, which has a bunch of mini gifts hidden inside:

  • Way longer context window (128K): developers can fit >300 pages of text into a single prompt, a 16X increase from before!

    • This also means us plebs will soon be able to input super long PDFs into ChatGPT, a feat once only reserved for Claude.

  • Way cheaper to use: 3X cheaper for input tokens and 2X cheaper for output tokens than GPT-4 (similar news for GPT-3.5 Turbo).

There are SO many more goodies for builders, like a new Assistants API, DALL-E 3 API, & Audio API. We’ll unpack all of these soon—stay tuned, ya cool cats!
