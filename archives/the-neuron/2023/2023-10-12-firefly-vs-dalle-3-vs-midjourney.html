Adobe Firefly vs. DALL-E 3 vs. Midjourney.

It’s 2023, and AI lets you whip up an image just from a description. It’s jaw-dropping, but so are smart fridges, which no one uses…

Two questions:

  1. Which image generators should you use?

  2. What should you use them for?

Midjourney was and still is the top option for hyperrealistic and abstract images. But there’s a steep learning curve and you're stuck using it within Discord.

There are two up-and-comers worth mentioning:

DALL-E 3 by OpenAI: Once a dud, now a stud. Worth using when your image needs text elements—think cartoons, branding, memes, you name it.

Adobe Firefly Image 2: this model just leveled up — it can do things like skin texture and hands quite well.

The big pro here is Adobe is a safe bet for businesses. Adobe doesn't train its AI on unauthorized art and just launched Content Credentials, a “digital nutrition label” that shows how an image was made and by whom.

(Firefly got a bunch of upgrades this week including photo upscaling and style alignment with your brand).

These image generators come in clutch for any project that involves visuals:

  • images for blog posts, social posts, or digital marketing.

  • product designs.

  • fashion/interior design.

  • diagrams/illustrations for educational content.

  • thumbnails (which you can now A/B test with ChatGPT-4V)!
