Spotify Introduces 'Siri For Music' 🎧

DJ, turn it up-up-up-up-up-up-up.

Yesterday, Spotify debuted DJ, a personalized music AI that serves users curated songs, artists, and commentary in a hyper-realistic voice.

DJ aims to create an unparalleled music-listening journey by harnessing the power of OpenAI's generative AI and Sonantic's dynamic AI voice. Here's how the tool works:

  1. DJ studies your playlists and songs to curate a fresh music lineup of new and old songs that it knows you'll love.

  2. The more you listen, the better its recommendations get (aka reinforcement training).

  3. The best part: if you don't like the vibe, just hit the DJ button, and you'll be transported to a new genre, artist, or mood.

Plus, DJ's spoken commentary is from the voice of an actual person named Xavier "X" Jernigan (Spotify's Head of Cultural Partnerships).

Bottom line: AI is set to revolutionize our favorite social apps, ushering in more personalized, passive, and (probably) addicting experiences. Music will be no exception.

The real question is whether DJ can recreate the same thrill of a banger blasting on the radio?!

Tome Raises $43 Million In AI Gold Rush

Who said we're in a bear market?!

Tome, an AI tool that transforms ideas into visual stories, just closed a $43 million Series B funding round at a $300 million valuation after launching less than six months ago.

Tome's demos are bound to take your breath away: the tool turns simple text prompts into "full visual narratives with intelligent layouts, copy and images."

  • On the backend, the interface combines collaborative AI with responsive design and interactive content to produce compelling visuals.

  • Users are leveraging Tome to craft design portfolios, lesson plans, slide decks, and much more.

Despite its marvel, Tome's $300 million price tag still signifies a considerable valuation for a company with no revenue to date.

Why it matters: As AI becomes 'the thing,' founders will start rushing to fundraise before VC dollars dry up.
