ChatGPT Plugins Are Live For Plus Users 🧲️

OpenAI is rolling out 70+ plugins to ChatGPT Plus users this week.

Up until now, AI has primarily served as a tool for generating content and code. Plugins make complex tasks more feasible.

As promised, we spent the weekend filtering through the nonsense threads on your feed (i.e., “1,000x productivity with these plugins”) to find the ones truly worth using:

  1. ChatWithPDF/AskYourPDF: upload and ask questions about your documents. Big upgrade, but some users are reporting that it’s buggy.

  2. Browsing: Finally access the internet with this one (no more September 2021 cutoff). Also huge, but it takes way longer than a regular Google search.

  3. Zapier: This can execute tasks across your workflows without leaving the interface, like sending emails, creating events, and updating spreadsheets.

All of these tools are beneficial, but we're uncertain how they'll fare against business platforms that are quickly incorporating AI into their own products.

Google, for instance, demoed products last week that compete across each of these three areas (Tailwind for files, AI Search, and Duet AI).

Why it matters: While ChatGPT still reigns in quality — we got early access to Duet in Gmail and found it pretty limited in customizability — companies capable of integrating AI into their ecosystems have a big distribution advantage.
