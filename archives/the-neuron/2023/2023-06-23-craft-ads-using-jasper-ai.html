How To Write Ads Using Jasper AI* ✍️

An eye-catching ad could be the difference between acquiring users at $2 or $10 apiece. The deciding factor?

One or two fleeting seconds to grab someone's attention before they keep scrolling.

Jasper is a time-saver for marketers allergic to prompt engineering. We’ve found their pre-made templates to be super helpful!

Here’s how to whip up social media ads in less than 5 minutes using Jasper!

  1. Sign up for Jasper's 7-day free trial (you can bail at anytime).

  2. Click "+ Create new content..."

  3. Hit "+ New from template."

  4. Select the "Facebook ad headline" template.

  5. Next, feed Jasper info on the ad you want. We recommend adding specific information about your brand’s tone where it says "Get started”.

  1. Then click "generate." Don't hesitate to click again for a fresh batch of examples.

Now it’s time to write the ‘damn ad! Navigate back to the "+ New from template" option. This time, select "Facebook ad primary text."

Rinse and repeat steps 1-6.

Presto! Mix and match the headlines and body text that resonates most and you've just created an entire ad that you can use across Facebook, Twitter, LinkedIn, TikTok, IG, and beyond.
