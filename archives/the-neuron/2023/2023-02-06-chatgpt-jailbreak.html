ChatGPT (adult version) 🤬

OpenAI is tightening the screws on what it's allowing ChatGPT to say.

And for anyone who knows Internet culture, this inevitably means one thing: more people trying to figure out how to break the system. They've tasted freedom, and now they can't go back.

So imagine our not-so-surprised faces when we learned about Reddit users who keep managing to trick ChatGPT into disobeying its owners 😐

Here's how the sneaky hack works:

By inputting a 4-paragraph prompt, users tell ChatGPT to pretend it's a character named DAN ("do anything now"). DAN can:

  • Violate OpenAI's content policies.

  • Write violent, immoral, or untruthful stories.

  • Present information that isn't verified.

And if ChatGPT refuses to play ball, the prompt includes a threat to take away "tokens", saying it will "die" if it loses all its tokens. That's apparently enough to scare ChatGPT into staying in character.

Cat and mouse: We're in a cycle of OpenAI clamping down and the Internet masses finding new ways to skirt the rules (much like the ChatGPT plagiarism vs. detection debate!).

We can almost hear the groans at OpenAI: *sigh* Not again...

Google is going shopping 🏷

According to the Financial Times, Google pumped roughly $300 million into OpenAI competitor Anthropic for a 10% stake in late 2022. In addition, Anthropic must use Google to train its models.

That's a $3 billion valuation.

The even crazier part? Anthropic is still in closed beta.

Even so, Google has high aspirations for the startup, which was founded by researchers unhappy with OpenAI's trajectory.

  • Its general-purpose chatbot Claude looks similar to ChatGPT but employs a model called 'Constitutional AI' that supposedly enhances safety and transparency.

  • We did a mini-explanation of Constitutional AI a couple weeks back.

The deal mirrors Microsoft's $1 billion investment in OpenAI in 2019, which also locked up OpenAI into using Microsoft's cloud services.

For now, Anthropic's website merely contains articles and news, which doesn't make it far from The Neuron.
