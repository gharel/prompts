Meta’s New Revolutionary Speech Model 🗣

Call Meta a five-star Michelin restaurant, ‘cause they just keep delivering the goods.

This time, it’s a speech AI named “Voicebox,” which uses an innovative approach called Flow Matching to perform audio tasks in ways it was never designed to.

Here’s what we mean:

text input (“welcome, humans”) + reference audio (Taylor Swift melody) = Taylor Swift singing “welcome, humans”.

Basically, mix any text with any audio (or omit features) to create any linguistic combination. Here are some of the amazing possibilities:

  • Create audio in the unique voice of those who are unable to speak.

  • Transform audio from English to Spanish while preserving the same voice.

  • Remove background noise or unnecessary words from any audio clip.

And here’s one not-so-amazing application: indistinguishable deepfakes of global leaders threatening nuclear attacks. Well, that escalated quickly.

For this reason, Meta isn’t sharing the Voicebox model or code with the public just yet.

Why it matters: The possibilities are endless. Personalized voice assistants. Content localization. Authentic voiceovers. Recordings free of background noise.

We anticipate companies to dish out large tips once this technology hits the market!

How To Captivate Your Audience Using Zapier & AI

Last week, our LinkedIn post was viewed over 4 million times. Our secret?

Crafting an irresistible hook. Here’s how we employed the power of Zapier & Excel to make it happen:

  1. We populated column #1 with viral Twitter/LinkedIn posts.

  2. In column #2, we jotted down bullet points for new posts.

  3. Finally, we sent a "Zap" to ChatGPT, instructing it to generate hooks in column #3 for our fresh ideas using the successful formats from column #1.

The result: an endless list of engaging hooks inspired by the most successful content on the internet.
