AI Deepfakes Are Getting Better & More Problematic.

On Monday, a vid of Mr Beast offering 10,000 iPhones for $2 each exploded on TikTok.

Unreal, right? That’s because it literally wasn’t real.

It’s the latest in AI scams going undetected by media platforms. Prior to this, it was a (deepfake) of Tom Hanks promoting a dental scam. And back in February, we covered a deepfake feat. Andrew Huberman.

It’s a bad look when the companies building AI can’t even spot AI-generated content on their own platforms. Akin to a dentist with yellow teeth.

Other AI companies like Google are working on solutions, but deepfakes are outpacing detection tools … and they are currently the worst they’ll ever be. No magic bullets here, but check out this piece for more insights.

Anthropic Might Raise At A $20-30B Valuation.

Money isn’t free in life—unless you're an AI startup, apparently.

Anthropic, the creator of Claude, could raise $2 billion from Amazon and Google at a staggering valuation of $20-30B.

Sure, Claude is dope — we use it to summarize 100-page PDFs — but at $100M revenue/year, the new price tag puts it at 200x revenue!

(Meanwhile, Apple’s at 7x - though it’s not exactly an apples-to-Anthropic comparison)

Coincidence much?

This huge valuation comes a week after OpenAI’s suggested valuation range of $80-90B, a 3x increase from its April valuation. Anthropic might be getting a little “Hey, us too?” action here.

Why this matters: While we're obviously excited about AI, we can't ignore the hype bubble swelling up in Silicon Valley-land.

A lot of it is justified, and a lot of it is not. The Information reported that many AI consumer apps that raised 9 figures are struggling to retain users and compete with ChatGPT.

PSA: Technology adoption always has its ups and downs. We just hope AI’s “downs” aren’t exacerbated by splurging.
