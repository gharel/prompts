Slower: Autonomous Agents.

A few months ago, people asked, “Why just tell ChatGPT what to do? What if we let it decide?” It sorta worked, and people went nuts imagining the future.

If ChatGPT was going from a bike to a car, then these autonomous agents are like going from a car to a self-driving car.

It’s now clear we’re in very early innings with agents. Startups are shifting away from letting AI run wild. After all, Google’s been working on self-driving cars for 14 years.

Still, building autonomous agents is the ultimate engineer’s dream. While you won’t be seeing AI run your business for you anytime soon (sorry!), it’s not going to stop the builders from trying to make it happen.
