AI Is Coming To Your Discord Server 📟

Discord is the Wild West of social media: think crypto degenerates, niche gamer communities, and, yup, cats eating pizza. Oh, and if you have a notifications phobia, definitely stay away.

Now, the $15 billion platform is leveraging AI to make its interface more family-friendly. Join a new server, and you'll discover:

1/ A ChatGPT-powered assistant, Clyde: Think ChatGPT but inside of an app. Ask it questions, get recommendations, and just vibe with your invisible friend.

*Did you know that a group of cats is called a "clowder"?!*

2/ Upgraded moderation: By integrating OpenAI tech, Discord's policing tool AutoMod is becoming even more automatic in detecting trolls and rule-breakers.

3/ AI-generated conversation summaries: Discord is an app that never sleeps. With overviews of the most important conversations, you'll never miss a beat.

Discord is also launching an incubator to fund developers leveraging AI to improve the platform.

Why it matters: As AI continues to permeate communication workflows, it's quietly turning its attention to social media. In fact, it might be the filter that lets us cut through the clutter and uncover the information that truly matters.

A Peek Into The Future Of AI Driving 🏍

Imagine this: instead of glancing down at your iPhone for directions, everything you need — navigation, safety alerts, travel data — is right on your windscreen.

That's what Envisics is building. The U.K. AI startup just raised a fresh $50 million in funding at a $500 million valuation for its holographic car technology.

For one, it's awesome: distracting car screens would disappear, and driving would become much safer.

But it's also far-fetched: adoption of new car tech has always been hindered by obstacles, and $500 million for a startup without a commercial product seems a tad extravagant.

We even have more customers than they do :)

Although still speculative, it provides a glimpse into the future, and any technology that harnesses AI warrants our attention.
