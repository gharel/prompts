Claude Supports 75,000 Words Of Context ⛽️

My least favorite sentence: “ChatGPT is at capacity.”

My 2nd least favorite sentence: “The message you submitted was too long.”

Yesterday, Anthropic announced a huge upgrade: Claude, its chatbot, can now process up to 75,000 words of context (3x more than OpenAI). That’s roughly 100 Wikipedia entries.

This means that Claude will be able to digest, summarize and reference information from hundreds of pages in seconds. To demonstrate this, Anthropic fed the magical model an 85-page Netflix corporate filing and it:

  • Highlighted important terms for investors

  • Created well-formatted tables from Netflix’s balance sheet.

  • Wrote analyses on Netflix’s market positioning.

This is work that would take a traditional analyst several days and an excessive amount of lattes on your company’s dime.

The possibilities are endless. Imagine no longer spending hours sorting through annual reports, legal documents, codebases, and and legislation. Just ask Claude.

And what happens when Claude can generate not just text but graphs, images, and entire PowerPoints from a PDF. Could a single analyst accomplish the work of ten with a Claude login?

Why it matters: The feature truly makes Anthropic a real competitor to OpenAI - plus, it just killed a dozen “chat with your PDF” tools. We wonder if OpenAI will one-up Anthropic with an even larger context window soon…

Generative AI Comes To Meta Advertisers ✨

With ChatGPT, every marketers new favorite sentence is: “Write me 10 versions of the following headline/copy/subject line.”

Now, they can perform this task directly on their advertising platform.

Meta announced a new product named “AI Sandbox” designed to save advertisers time when crafting ad creatives.

The Sandbox offers three main features:

  1. Generates multiple variations of ad copy.

  2. Creates diverse assets for your campaigns.

  3. Cropping tool to customize visuals.

We personally haven’t gotten our feet dirty in the Sandbox yet (it’s set to expand in July), but the prospect of eliminating writer’s block is indeed enticing.

Why it matters: Meta is striving to fulfill its promise that AI is its focus, not useless headsets. With its distribution, we think it’s well-positioned to become the go-to for AI in advertising.
