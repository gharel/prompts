Inside OpenAI's First Round Picks

OpenAI's got a fantastic deal for you.

You let them invest, and you get first access to their new APIs (including GPT-4), Microsoft Azure credits and access to OpenAI researchers.

It was a no-brainer for OpenAI's first set of investments. The AI lab has invested in at least 12 companies using its $100 million fund.

Here's the list and what they're building:

1/ Anysphere - Still in stealth. Claiming to build "AI tools for professionals".

2/ Atomic Semi - A faster, cheaper way to build semiconductors.

3/ Cursor - An AI-first code editor.

4/ Diagram - Putting AI in Figma.

5/ Harvey - Copilot or AI assistant for lawyers.

6/ Kick - AI-driven bookkeeping.

7/ Milo - An AI assistant that helps parents organize the chaos.

8/ qqbot.dev - GitHub Copilot, except it can understand your entire code base.

9/ EdgeDB - An open-source database technology.

10/ Mem Labs - A popular note-taking app driven by AI.

11/ Speak - An AI-powered language tutor for English.

12/ Descript - Audio/video editing platform.

OpenAI also funds a 5-week accelerator, where it takes 10% of the companies participating. The first one just ended, so more companies are on the way.

How to make money: Build a super powerful AI, help startups become the next billion-dollar companies, take 10%+ along the way.

AI + 4chan = Deeply Problematic. Who Knew? 😐

Totally not expected at all.

Startup ElevenLabs is building AI-generated voices. They're starting with a voice simulator: upload your voice, and the AI can make you say anything.

Cue 4chan brain: "How about we ignore the intended usage and use this to make celebrities say racist and violent things?"

ElevenLabs has had to clamp down on its beta product and consider locking down new user registration, much like OpenAI requires a phone number to register.

"Silly child", says Microsoft. Their voice-cloning model VALL-E blew up in the headlines, but they stopped short of releasing it publicly. They probably saw this coming.

ChatGPT With Chinese Characteristics 🇨🇳

Baidu says, "Me too!"

Just like its American cousin Google was put on notice after ChatGPT, Baidu noticed Chinese users spilling out of China's firewalls to get their hands on the AI tool.

Google sees 6-7 times more searches for ChatGPT from China than from the US, even though Google is banned and ChatGPT forbids Chinese users.

So they're building their own ChatGPT (name TBD) into their search engine in March.

It'll be built off of Ernie, the language model Baidu first released in Dec 2021 and cutely named after Google's BERT model.
