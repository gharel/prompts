GPT-4 Outperforms Humans On Pitch Decks 🧑‍🏫

At first, we thought this article was from The Onion. “GPT-4 Outperforms Humans in Pitch Deck Effectiveness.”

There was no way AI could tell a more compelling story than humans filled with emotional baggage, right? Right?!

Wrong. VC firm Clarify Capital had hundreds of investors rate pitch decks written by a/ humans and b/ GPT-4. 

Investors were:

  • 3x more likely to tag GPT-4 decks as "high quality”

  • 3x more likely to invest in the GPT pitch decks.

And these stats were consistent for pitches across finance, marketing, and finance!

Sure, GPT-4 won’t be presenting in Shark Tank anytime soon, but ChatGPT could be a major secret weapon for anyone pitching an idea, proposal, or product at work.

Here’s a cheat sheet for ya:

  1. Be crystal clear about what you want (e.g., we need more investment).

  2. Feed ChatGPT successful pitches (e.g., watch this Steve Jobs keynote).

  3. Specify target audience and tone (e.g., marketing director, persuasive tone).

Important Insights From McKinsey’s Automation Report 👇

Okay, fine: AI probably didn’t whip up this 68-page report on the state of automation.

It’s become clear that generative AI will hit knowledge workers big time, but a new McKinsey report sheds light on the types of knowledge workers that’ll see the most impact.

Put simply, AI will invade jobs with a clear ‘if-this-then-that.’ The types of broad types of jobs that you could train a remote summer intern how to do:

  • dealing with customers.

  • creating marketing content

  • executing sales funnels.

  • software engineering tasks.

There are more layers to this AI onion, especially when we talk about the specific functions that AI can handle within these areas.

But three industries that will derive the most value from automating these functions are banking, CPG, & pharma. A random group, we know.

A few quick hits from this (way too long) PDF:

  • AI could massively boost labor productivity.

  • Automation will be faster in developed countries.

  • AI with “social and emotional” expertise is coming way sooner than we thought.
