ChatGPT...With Pictures 🖍

ChatGPT just learned how to see.

Microsoft just introduced Visual ChatGPT, a chatbot that generates, edits, and explains images from text prompts. 

ChatGPT can only spit out text, while other AI models only do images or music or so on.

With our powers combined. Now, Microsoft has plugged ChatGPT into image models, so even if ChatGPT can't do images, it can call in those image models for help.

  • For example, ask Visual ChatGPT for a picture of a cat, then ask it to replace the cat with a dog. Dunno why you'd replace the cat, but you get the point.

  • You can also ask Visual ChatGPT questions about a picture: what's in there, colors, styles, etc.

This AI + AI contraption is called multimodal AI: it can take in and spit out text and images and videos and audio, not just one.

Leading companies like You.com, Bing, and OpenAI have said that this is the future of generative AI. The challenge lies in how well models like ChatGPT can integrate other models as resources.

What's next: ChatGPT is just version 1.0. Soon, you'll be able to generate (& edit) any creative output just from a simple text prompt.

GPT-4 Reportedly Set To Release This Week 🙀

If you think Visual ChatGPT is cool, wait to see what else GPT-4 can perform.

The upgraded model is set to be released this week, according to Dr. Andreas Braun, CTO of Microsoft Germany. 

So far, we've heard mixed opinions. Some of our sources with beta access are seriously impressed by GPT-4, while others are "meh" about it.

  • Braun says GPT-4 will be multimodal, but others aren't sure if he misspoke. If it is, GPT-4 could excel in unprecedented ways on visual IQ tests, speech recognition, and image understanding, as we saw with Visual ChatGPT.

  • Startups funded by OpenAI are already using GPT-4, given their inside-track access.

  • We've heard it's better at math, but still far off from perfect.

Here's Nvidia research scientist & Stanford PhD Jim Fan on GPT-4:

"What matters is that you need to brace yourself or your organization for multimodal LLM APIs. Heed my words - this is an unstoppable force and will inevitably come - very likely before the end of 2023."

We can't tell what makes us trust Fan more: Nvidia AI Scientist or Stanford PhD?
