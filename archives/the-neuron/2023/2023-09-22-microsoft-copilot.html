Microsoft’s AI Assistant For Work Is Coming.

Satya Nadella LIVE!

Microsoft’s AI assistant for everything is coming soon, and we spoke with Microsoft vets Yusuf Mehdi and Colette Stallbaumer about what it all means for its 345M paying users.

(that’s right, 345,000,000 — more than the entire population of the US).

The game-changer is that Microsoft 365 Chat can interact with all your work apps.

Here’s a peak at what this could actually look like:

  1. Summarize unread emails from [Pete].

  2. Draft a reply to [Pete] saying “thanks, received pres info for AMA.” and make it sound like me.

  3. Open draft in Outlook and change tone to informal. Send.

  4. Create a task in [Microsoft Loop] for presentation.

  5. Draft an event plan in Word based on [Pete’s email for our AMA].

  6. Turn [Word doc] into a [PowerPoint] using our company template.

  7. Change the header image to something with more pizzazz.

  8. Draft email to [Pete], attach [Powerpoint], and schedule team meeting tomorrow at 11am.

In essence, a super-smart assistant that's deeply integrated into your work ecosystem, predicting your needs with suggested prompts and actions.

Mind-blowing … and it actually exists (unlike some products stuck in demo video phase, ahem ahem Duet AI). We even had Microsoft employees demo it for us live.

Copilot will hit enterprises on November 1st for $30/user/month. In the meantime, businesses that use Microsoft must get their teams up to speed on what this means and how to use it.
