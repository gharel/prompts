Two Ways to Play 🇺🇸 🇪🇺

The age-old question: to regulate or not to regulate?

Washington finds itself at the center of a tug of war between consumer advocates and tech entrepreneurs.

So far, we're seeing more words than actions.

  • President Biden has (only) met with tech leaders to emphasize the industry's responsibility to guardrail products before releasing them (i.e., Microsoft).

  • In a similar vein, the FTC put tech companies on notice that there will be repercussions if they exaggerate the capabilities of their AI products.

This week a delegation of lawmakers met with tech execs In Silicon Valley. Their takeaway: some regulation is needed, but America still needs to maintain its technological advantage over China.

For now, it's a 'wait-and-see' approach.

This is unlike the European Union, which is working hard to pass a comprehensive legal framework that would put the clamps on AI development through its bill the Artificial Intelligence Act.

Why it matters: The US is quickly becoming the tamest environment for developing new AI systems. Let's just hope its sluggish regulatory approach to regulation doesn't have consequences down the line.

Stability AI Is Burning Cash 🔥

Stability AI, once among the trendiest AI startups in Silicon Valley, is quickly burning through its $100 million bankroll as it struggles to generate revenue.

  • The company took the world by storm with its introduction of Stability Diffusion last summer.

  • Now, its frantic and largely unorganized operations are making it hard to raise its new round, which would value the company at a whopping $4 billion.

Our take: Stability AI plays an important role in the AI ecosystem, but it needs to stay alive:

  • Stability's "open source first" approach could keep the world free from an AI industry monopolized by 1-2 players.

  • Yet, building a business around open source is hard - though obviously not impossible - since you give away a big part of your tech away for free.

  • Semafor's reporting of Stability's "radical independence" (read: no plan?) matches what we've heard on the ground. That's not encouraging when the cards are stacked against you.

We think Stability AI has a big opportunity but needs to move into wartime mode - and fast.
