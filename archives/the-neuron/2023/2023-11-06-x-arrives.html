Elon’s ChatGPT Rival Is Finally Here.

Elon Musk’s ChatGPT alternative “Grok” is finally here—for cozy friends and insiders, of course.

Grok is somewhere in between ChatGPT-3 and GPT-3.5 in capability.

So while impressively efficient having just been trained on 33B parameters over two months, Grok is definitely a ways behind ChatGPT-4.

But it’s pretty darn unique from anything we’ve seen.

For one, it has a stand-out personality. Like, the type of energy you get at a NYC comedy club at 2am (see here for proof). So Grok definitely sounds the most humanlike from any chatbot we’ve seen, which is a +1.

It’s also an open book, designed to answer “almost anything.” This diverges from ChatGPT's very cautious approach to potentially harmful content—a point Musk has vocally criticized.

It pulls the latest info from Elon’s other playground X/Twitter so you get real-time information. Clever. Conveniently, Grok is currently available to anyone paying $16/month for X Premium+.

Here’s our take: It’s never a bad idea to doubt Elon Musk latest ambitious projects—at least not publicly—but Grok is starting way behind in the race considering GPT-3 was trained half a decade ago.

The real-time knowledge via X Twitter is definitely a pro, but it has a lot of work to do to catch up to ChatGPT-4’s IQ and reasoning.

Grok will also be making its way into Teslas, so users can feel sustainable while cheffing up cocaine in the back seat!

This Unexpected Prompting Technique Actually Works.

Despite ChatGPT not being human … it is designed to mimic human intelligence … so treating it as though it possesses emotions … could actually yield better outcomes.

This 32-page study is proof.

Researchers discovered that tacking on “emotional stimuli” to ChatGPT prompts can improve performance.

Which sound like this:

  • “this is very important for my career”.

  • “you’d better be sure”.

  • “your commitment to excellence sets you apart”.

In layman’s terms: put ChatGPT under pressure and hype up its abilities, like you’re coaching a high school football team.
