Leaders Call For Immediate Pause On Training AI Past GPT-4 😱

If you think AI is moving too fast, you're not alone.

Prominent leaders, including Elon Musk, Steve Wozniak, and Andrew Yang, are calling for an immediate pause on training AI systems beyond GPT-4 for at least six months.

Their argument: AI is becoming too powerful, and the proper safety protocols are lacking. Plus, the rush to ship AI products ASAP (hi, Microsoft!) has only made matters worse:

"...recent months have seen AI labs locked in an out-of-control race to develop and deploy ever more powerful digital minds that no one – not even their creators – can understand, predict, or reliably control."

Pause Giant AI Experiments: An Open Letter

It's a bold proposal that has been met with a healthy amount of skepticism online.

The counterarguments:

  • Pausing research is impractical and only enforceable if the government steps in

  • We're starting to make huge progress in education and healthcare via AI

  • China sure ain't slowing down

Our take: We don't think this goes anywhere. It's targeted at OpenAI (which Elon wants to compete with), and even Elon has said: "Leading AGI developers will not heed this warning."

Bing Chat Is Open For Advertising 💬

Microsoft is bringing ads to Bing Chat.

The company announced in a blog post that ads will be incorporated into Bing's chat outputs as 'Ad' citations. It's also exploring ways to share ad revenue with the actual content contributors.

  • It's a small win for publishers, who now exist at the mercy of LLMs that gobble up and repurpose their content.

  • ChatGPT, for instance, doesn't cite or share revenue with web publishers whose content it scrapes and uses for outputs.

Why it matters: Microsoft CFO Amy Hood courted investors by saying that every 1% of market share in search advertising is worth $2 billion in revenue. Time to see if Bing Chat can deliver.
