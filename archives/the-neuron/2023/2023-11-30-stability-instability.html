Stability AI is an amazing startup but not so amazing business.

never good when a founder is labeled “internet personality”

Yin vs. yang. Accelerationists vs. doomers. A visionary startup founder vs. ROI-driven VCs.

Meet Emad Mostaque.

He’s the controversial CEO of Stability AI, a hot startup that has seen more ups and downs than a Real Housewives reunion episode.

The good news is: Stability AI is shipping incredible AI products.

They’ve got their flagship image generator SDXL 1.0, which IMHO takes 3rd place behind Midjourney and DALL-E 3, plus:

  • Stable Audio: create music from text prompts.

  • SDXL Turbo (NEW): instantaneous image generation (<1 second).

  • Stable Video (NEW): turns images into short videos (like this Guy Looking Back meme!).

  • Plus 10+ nifty editing tools like swapping items, upscaling, replacing backgrounds, and more (all FREE on Clipdop).

The incredible thing about Stability is that it open-sources its image model, meaning anyone can build products on top of it (unlike Midjourney/OpenAI).

The bad news is: when you’re making free stuff, it’s really hard to make money.

This is why Stability failed to raise money at a $4B valuation (!) earlier this year—it ended up settling for $25M and $50M convertibles notes in June and November.

To make matters worse, CEO Mostaque is wanted gone by investors after prompting a staff exodus and being exposed as a rampant liar via Forbes’ brutal exposé.

Now, there were whispers that Stability might try to get acquired.
